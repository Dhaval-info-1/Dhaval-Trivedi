Vision Transformers: The Next Leap in Computer Vision

How transformer models are revolutionizing image understanding and computer vision tasks.

Pillar: Foundations | Cluster: Vision | Subcluster: Transformers

Introduction

Vision Transformers (ViTs) have brought the power of transformer architectures to computer vision, enabling new levels of accuracy and flexibility.

How Vision Transformers Work

  • Patch Embedding: Images are split into patches and embedded as tokens.
  • Self-Attention: Enables the model to focus on relevant image regions.
  • Transfer Learning: Pre-trained ViTs can be fine-tuned for various vision tasks.

Applications

ViTs are used in image classification, object detection, and even generative art.
