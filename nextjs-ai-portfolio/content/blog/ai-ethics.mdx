# AI Ethics: Navigating the Moral Landscape

As AI systems become more pervasive, ethical considerations are paramount. This article discusses the challenges and frameworks for responsible AI.

## Key Ethical Issues
- Bias and fairness
- Transparency
- Accountability

## Frameworks for Ethical AI
- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Human-in-the-loop systems
- Regulatory compliance

## Final Thoughts
Ethical AI is not just a technical challenge but a societal imperative. Developers, policymakers, and users must collaborate to ensure AI benefits all.
